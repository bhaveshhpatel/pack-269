How to enroll a new scout or renew for a returning scout

New Scouts

  1. Come to a meeting. Visit with parents and leaders to see if Scouting is for your family. Families can “try before you buy” by attending meetings and activities, but registration and uniforms are expected by the end of Fall.
  2. Enroll with Scouting America and pay National and Council fees. This is required to be registered as a Scout and covered by BSA insurance during scouting activities.
  3. Submit the Pack 269 Application form (see Application Forms) and pay Pack fees (checks payable to Pack 269) to a pack leader. Pack fees fund the cost of running Pack activities.
  4. Purchase uniform and handbook. Uniforms are available at Scout shops or online — see our What to Buy guide. We recommend buying in person to check sizing (and size up so it lasts more than a year). The closest Scout shops are in Pleasanton and Palo Alto.
  5. Consider becoming a leader. Cub Scouting is a family program and we encourage parents to get involved. Registered leaders receive a discount on Pack fees.

Returning Scouts

  1. Renew and pay National / Council fees before your annual renewal date at my.scouting.org.
  2. Pay Pack fees by submitting a check (payable to Pack 269) and an application form (see Application Forms) to a Pack leader.

General Information

All Pack leaders are unpaid volunteers. Pack fees are used to pay for activity materials, meeting room and campground rent, and awards for scouts — none of it goes to leaders.

It is the policy of Pack 269 to help families in cases where paying registration fees is a financial hardship. Please let a Pack leader know if that is the case — contact us at pack269info@gmail.com.
