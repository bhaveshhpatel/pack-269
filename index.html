Welcome to Pack 269!

A dynamic and adventurous group of young scouts in Fremont, CA — learning, growing, and having fun together.

Join Pack 269 See Upcoming Events

Pack 269 proudly welcomes boys and girls from kindergarten through fifth grade from Parkmont Elementary and other surrounding schools in the area. Our pack is rooted in the values of the Cub Scout program, fostering a sense of community, leadership, and responsibility among our members. Whether we’re exploring the great outdoors, working on exciting projects, or participating in community service, Pack 269 is all about building character, making friends, and creating lasting memories.

Our pack meetings and activities take place at the Veterans Memorial Hall in Niles, providing a central and historic location for our scouts to gather, learn, and have fun together. Whether you’re part of Parkmont or another nearby school, we invite you to join us in the adventure of Cub Scouting!
